# Call for Expressions of Interest (EOI): ABACBS 2026 & BiocAsia 2026 Workshops

The ABACBS 2026 Organising Committee, in collaboration with the BiocAsia 2026 Organising Committee, invites proposals for specialized workshops to be held in conjunction with the annual conference.

Workshops are a core, highly popular feature of the ABACBS annual meeting. They provide a brilliant platform for researchers, industry experts, and educators to share technical expertise, introduce novel software/tools, and lead practical, hands-on sessions for the community. We encourage submissions covering a diverse range of foundational and cutting-edge topics, including but not limited to:

1. Long Reads
2. High-throughput sequencing data analysis
3. Single-cell and spatial omics
4. Microbiome & Metagenomics
5. Scalable and reproducible workflows
6. Compute infrastructure and cloud computing
7. Machine learning and AI in genomics

## Workshop Format & Logistics

- **Date:** 19 November 2026 (Afternoon Session) and 20 November 2026 (Full Day)
- **Duration:** Proposals can be for 1.5 hour short sessions, half-day sessions (approx. 3 hours excluding breaks), or full-day sessions (approx. 6 hours excluding breaks).
- **Delivery Mode:** In-person / Hybrid (to be confirmed)
- **Target Audience:** Content can range from introductory (beginner) to advanced technical levels. Please explicitly state any prerequisites beforehand.

## Proposal Submission Requirements

To submit your EOI, please prepare a single document containing the following details and send it to the workshop organisers listed under [Further enquiries](#further-enquiries) below:

- **Workshop Title:** Clear, descriptive, and appealing to potential attendees.
- **Organizer(s) & Presenter(s):** Names, affiliations, contact emails, and brief biographies (max 100 words per person) demonstrating expertise in the topic area.
- **Abstract & Objectives:** A summary of the workshop content and what participants will learn (max 300 words).
- **Format & Duration:** 1.5 hours / 3 hours / 6 hours. Please outline a rough schedule or breakdown of topics.
- **Target Audience & Prerequisites:** Explain who should attend and any technical requirements (e.g., basic familiarity with R/Python, Unix command line, specific software pre-installations, or account creations). Mention if your workshop is about a Bioconductor tool or framework.
- **Maximum Capacity:** The maximum number of participants your teaching team can comfortably support for hands-on troubleshooting.

## Evaluation Criteria

The ABACBS 2026 Program Committee will evaluate submissions based on:

1. Relevance and timeliness of the topic to the ABACBS community.
2. Clarity of the learning outcomes and structural feasibility of the schedule.
3. Presenter experience and ability to deliver interactive, hands-on content.
4. Balanced representation of topics across the overall conference workshop program.

**Note on high demand:** Workshop slots are limited and historically highly competitive. Organizers may occasionally be asked to modify or shorten their sessions to fit the final program scheduling.
